Evaluation Metrics - Face Distance Score
●
●
●

For each generated face, we find its average distance to training data
and average this distance across all generated faces.
Distances between faces determined by a neural network, GhostFaceNet
Since we want a model that generates a specific person, less average
distance is better.

Paper reference: GhostFaceNets
