Image Generation Strategies (Autoregressive
vs. Non-Autoregressive Models)
4 students per group
Round 1: Autoregressive(AR) Generation (4 minutes)
• Choose one student as the “generator” (writer/artist).
• The rest of the group acts as the prompt and evaluators.
• Give the generator a prompt — e.g.,
• “Draw a dog running on grass.”
• “Write a short 4-line poem about AI.”
• The generator must produce one element at a time (one word, one shape, or one
part of the drawing), waiting for approval before continuing.
• No going back or editing earlier parts (like token-by-token text generation).
• Other team members can only give minimal hints like “keep going.”

Round 2: Non-Autoregressive(NAR) Generation (4 minutes)
• Now all group members create simultaneously - each one draws or
writes a different part at the same time, without coordination/discussion.
• For example:
• One draws the head of the dog, another draws the body of the dog,
another draws the grass, another adds background, etc.
• Or for text: each person writes one line of a poem at the same time.

Group Reflection
Each team discusses:
• Which version was faster?
• Which version looked or read more coherent?
• What challenges did you face in Round 2?
• How might “making all positions imagine the same thing” help solve
inconsistency?

Conclusion:
- AR models produce more consistent but slower
results
- NAR models generate faster but can lose coherence.
