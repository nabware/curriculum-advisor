Hallucination
• Definition: A confident response generated by an AI system that
appears plausible but is factually incorrect, fabricated, or
unsupported by real data.
• Types of hallucination:
• Factual hallucination: false statements about real-world facts.
• Logical hallucination : invalid reasoning or contradictions.
• Attribution hallucination: citing nonexistent sources or misquoting.
